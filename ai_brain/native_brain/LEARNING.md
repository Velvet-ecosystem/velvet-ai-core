# Native Brain Learning Boundary

Velvet's first learning integration is proposal-only.

The Native Brain may gather flagged reflection reviews and produce an immutable `LearningProposal`. The proposal preserves links to its source reviews and receipts, names the candidate subject, and records whether enough evidence exists to justify later review.

It does not apply a change.

## Allowed

- gather flagged reflection records
- preserve evidence links
- create a bounded candidate subject
- record proposed or insufficient-evidence disposition
- present the proposal for later human or governed review

## Forbidden

- changing model weights or embeddings
- editing prompts, policy, thresholds, or code
- altering memory or continuity records
- granting permissions or selecting executors
- publishing commands or actuation requests
- treating repeated evidence as automatic approval
- promoting its own proposal

## Promotion boundary

A future promotion path must be explicit, separately reviewed, physically present where required, receipted, reversible, and unable to bypass Runtime or Court.

> Learning may propose. It may not quietly become behavior.
