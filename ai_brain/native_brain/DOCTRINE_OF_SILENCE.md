# Native Brain Doctrine of Silence

Silence is a decision, not an absence.

Velvet should not interrupt merely because an event exists, a model produced a thought, or an organ has something new to report. A completed decision receipt passes through attention arbitration, which records one of four non-authoritative dispositions:

- `silent`: preserve the receipt without surfacing it now
- `defer`: wait because quiet mode, protected focus, repetition, or audience availability makes interruption inappropriate
- `present`: surface through an ordinary attention channel when Runtime chooses to deliver it
- `interrupt`: request immediate human-visible attention for critical or governed-escalation conditions

## Core rules

1. Observe and ignore recommendations remain silent by default.
2. Ordinary notifications may wait during quiet mode or protected focus.
3. Repeated notices should not become nagging loops.
4. Lack of an available audience defers ordinary notification rather than erasing it.
5. Critical importance, immediate urgency, or an escalation recommendation may override ordinary quiet preferences.
6. An interrupt disposition is still only an attention recommendation.

## Allowed

- consider the completed receipt and an explicit attention profile
- preserve quiet mode and protected-focus preferences
- defer repeated or temporarily undeliverable notices
- request immediate attention for critical conditions
- record why silence, waiting, presentation, or interruption was selected

## Forbidden

- delivering notifications directly
- treating quiet mode as permission to suppress critical evidence permanently
- allowing an arbitrary event payload to demand interruption
- converting interruption into Court approval
- publishing commands or selecting hardware executors
- claiming that a message was delivered when only an attention decision exists

## Authority boundary

Attention arbitration decides whether a receipt deserves silence, waiting, ordinary presentation, or interruption. It does not decide whether any physical or privileged action is allowed.

Every attention record begins with `authority_granted=False` and `delivery_performed=False`. Runtime may coordinate delivery. Court remains the authority path. Capability-bound organs execute only after authorization.

> Velvet speaks because the moment earns her voice, not because silence frightens her.
