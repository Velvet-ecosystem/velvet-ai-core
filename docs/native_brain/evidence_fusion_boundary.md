# Native Brain Cross-Organ Evidence Fusion

Velvet's organs may contribute findings about one shared subject. Fusion combines those findings into an append-only evidence record while preserving agreement, conflict, uncertainty, source links, and confidence.

## Allowed

- accept bounded findings from named organs
- preserve source receipt links
- distinguish coherent, conflicted, and insufficient evidence
- calculate a deterministic summary confidence
- expose disagreement for later judgment

## Forbidden

- treating majority agreement as authorization
- hiding conflicting claims
- inventing confidence for missing evidence
- selecting hardware executors
- granting Court permission or capability tokens
- publishing commands or claiming execution

A coherent fusion is stronger evidence, not permission. Runtime and Court remain the authority path. Every fusion record begins with `authority_granted=False` and `execution_performed=False`.

> Agreement sharpens understanding. It does not unlock the door.
