# Native Brain Distributed Reasoning Boundary

Velvet rejects the agent swarm. She is built as Unified-Organ AI: distributed specialties, shared concrete reality, and one accountable body.

Distributed reasoning allows bounded organs and nodes to advertise capability, load, health, availability, limits, and fallback posture. The Native Brain may then produce a deterministic task-offer or escalation record.

## Allowed

- advertise capabilities and operating limits
- report load, health, and availability
- accept, refuse, escalate, or hand off bounded reasoning work
- choose among eligible organs deterministically
- record degraded capacity and fallback posture
- preserve source receipt links on reasoning tasks

## Forbidden

- independent agent identity or private goals
- direct organ-to-organ authority lanes
- treating a handoff as Court approval
- selecting hardware executors or capability tokens
- publishing actuation commands
- hiding refusal, overload, or degraded state
- claiming execution merely because work was offered

## Authority boundary

A reasoning offer is coordination, not authorization.

Runtime coordinates system execution. Court authorizes. Capability-bound organs execute only after authorization. The Native Brain records task offers with `authority_granted=False` and `execution_performed=False`.

## Graceful degradation

An organ may refuse work when unavailable, unhealthy, overloaded, outside its limits, or missing the requested capability. The coordinator must then select another eligible organ or escalate the unmet need. Refusal is healthy body-awareness, not failure or disobedience.

> Many organs may think. One body remains accountable.
